What this does

Identifies a stranded electrical conductor from a photo, by measuring its diameter against the printed marker card and reading its colour. Print the card at 100%, lay the conductor flat along the magenta line, and shoot from directly above.

Best results: fill the frame with the whole card, keep it flat, and shoot from ~25 cm using zoom rather than up close.

Decision-support prototype. Always confirm a critical size with calipers or the printed cable marking before relying on it.
